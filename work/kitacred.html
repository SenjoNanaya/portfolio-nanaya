Context

Thin-file microfinance applicants are hard to assess with traditional credit data alone. For BPI Data Wave 2025, Track 2, our team prototyped a PyTorch alternative-data trust score and presented it as an interactive explainable-AI mockup matched to the bank's stated needs. The team took 1st place in the track.

The team presented an interactive mockup to explain the assessment flow alongside the score.

Constraints

  • Hackathon demo and mockup, not a production credit system.
  • Applicant counts are sample data from the demo.
  • The mockup explains the assessment flow; it does not prove each score is correct.

What I did

As team leader, I directed a four-person team to the track title. I prototyped the alternative-data trust score, folded Eskwelabs mentor feedback into each iteration, and delivered a 3-step interactive mockup of the assessment flow for the final pitch.

Key decisions

  • Rebuilt the pitch around mentor feedback instead of defending the first version, realigning the mockup with what the bank said it needed.
  • Presented the score inside a 3-step explanation flow, so judges could follow how an assessment is reached.
  • Demonstrated the prototype using 50+ sample applicants.

Verification

Screened 50+ sample applicants in the final demo. Each iteration incorporated Eskwelabs mentor feedback before the next round.

Result

1st place, Track 2, BPI Data Wave Hackathon 2025, with a Python microfinance assessment prototype, a PyTorch trust score, and a 3-step interactive explanation mockup.

Limitation

This is a hackathon demo and mockup, not a production credit system. Applicant counts are sample data from the demo. The mockup does not prove each score is correct; the available evidence does not establish validation against repayment outcomes.

What I would do next

  • Evaluate the trust score against labelled repayment outcomes before considering real-world use.
  • Write down exactly where the mockup's explanations can mislead, before anyone relies on them.
  • Keep mentor review in the loop before any use with real applicants.
