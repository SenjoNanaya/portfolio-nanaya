Context

Pathag started as a hackathon entry for pedestrian accessibility on the UPLB campus: score campus walking routes for accessibility, and let people report obstacles. The team won collegiate champion at EXPE21ENCE YSES: The HackFest (Team Iterate).

Crowd-sourced reports are noisy. A route scorer that trusts every report will send people down bad paths, so verification had to be part of the design, not an afterthought.

Constraints

  • Hackathon prototype, not a deployed navigation service.
  • Auth and users modules are scaffolds.
  • Image classification results stay advisory until verified.

What I did

As team leader, I led a team of five to the collegiate title and built the backend core: ranked 5+ campus walking paths by accessibility, with 3 REST endpoints and a WebSocket channel for obstacle reports and crowd-sourced verification. I also integrated 6-class path-condition and binary obstacle classifiers plus the Flutter prototype client.

Key decisions

  • Gated route influence behind verification: obstacle reports affect scoring only after the verification threshold is met.
  • Kept image classification results advisory (eligible_for_live_map=false) until checked.
  • Separated the ML service from the routing service.

Verification

The prototype is designed to apply obstacle reports to route scoring only after the verification threshold is met. Image classification responses stay advisory until verified.

Result

A working prototype: accessibility-scored campus routes, REST and WebSocket endpoints for reports and verification, ML classifiers behind verifier gates, and a phone-app prototype client, presented to a collegiate-champion finish at the HackFest.

Limitation

This is a hackathon prototype, not a deployed service. The available project evidence does not establish how it performs on real commutes or prove that any route is safe.

What I would do next

  • Test with real accessibility volunteers on more campus routes and record where the scores disagree with them.
  • Expand coverage beyond the initial campus area before claiming anything about other places.
  • Replace the auth and users scaffolds before any pilot with real accounts.
